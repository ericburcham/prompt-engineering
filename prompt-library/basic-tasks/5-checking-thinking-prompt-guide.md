# Checking Your Thinking Prompt Guide: Storage Tank Allocation Strategy

## Summary

**Checking Your Thinking with M365 Copilot**  
Learn how to use AI as a strategic thought partner to validate operational decisions and identify potential blind spots in your reasoning.

## Explanation

This prompt demonstrates how to leverage M365 Copilot to review and challenge your operational strategy decisions.

The prompt works because it:
- Presents your current thinking clearly
- Asks for specific types of analysis and critique
- Requests identification of risks or oversights
- Leverages AI's ability to consider multiple perspectives
- Seeks quantitative validation where possible

## The Prompt

```
**TASK:**
Review my storage tank allocation strategy for our Houston terminal and identify potential flaws, risks, or optimization opportunities I may have overlooked. Provide a critical analysis with specific recommendations.

**CONTEXT:**
I am the Terminal Manager planning Q2 2025 tank allocations for our 5-million-barrel storage facility. Current market conditions show strong contango in crude oil ($3/bbl spread over 3 months) and weakening refined products demand. I'm considering shifting 1.2 million barrels from gasoline/diesel storage to crude oil storage to capture contango profits. This would leave us with 2.8M bbls crude, 1.5M bbls gasoline, 0.7M bbls diesel capacity.

**REFERENCES:**
My current reasoning:
1. Contango opportunity: $3/bbl × 1.2M bbls × 4 turns/year = $14.4M additional revenue
2. Refined products demand down 8% YoY in our region
3. We have excess gasoline tank capacity (65% utilization past 3 months)
4. Crude tanks running at 95% utilization
5. Tank cleaning and conversion would cost $2M and take 45 days
6. Our top 3 gasoline customers have termed contracts through Q4 2025

Assumptions I'm making:
- Contango structure will persist for at least 6 months
- Can find crude oil storage customers quickly
- Gasoline demand won't rebound suddenly
- $2M conversion cost is worth the revenue opportunity
- Operational flexibility loss is acceptable

Please analyze my strategy considering:
- Market risk scenarios
- Customer relationship impacts  
- Operational constraints I'm missing
- Financial model sensitivity
- Alternative strategies
- Historical precedents for similar decisions
```

## Evaluation

To evaluate Copilot's analysis:
- **Thoroughness** - Does it examine all key assumptions?
- **Specificity** - Are risks quantified where possible?
- **Balance** - Does it present both pros and cons fairly?
- **Actionability** - Are recommendations practical and specific?
- **Insight quality** - Does it surface non-obvious considerations?

## Iteration

To deepen the analysis:
- **Need more scenarios?** Add "Model impact if contango collapses within 90 days"
- **Customer focus?** Include "Analyze contract termination penalties and relationship risks"
- **Operational detail?** Add "Consider vapor recovery system compatibility and permit modifications"
- **Regional factors?** Specify "Include hurricane season tank availability requirements"
- **Strategic view?** Request "Compare against keeping flexibility for spot opportunities"
