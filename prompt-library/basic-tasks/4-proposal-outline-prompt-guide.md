# Proposal Outline Prompt Guide: Pipeline Expansion Project

## Summary

**Outlining Proposals with M365 Copilot**  
Learn how to create comprehensive proposal outlines for capital projects in the midstream oil and gas industry, leveraging M365's document structuring capabilities.

## Explanation

This prompt demonstrates how to outline a major pipeline expansion proposal using M365 Copilot.

The prompt works because it:
- Defines clear proposal objectives and scope
- Specifies stakeholder concerns to address
- Requests appropriate level of detail for each section
- Uses M365's strength in creating structured documents
- Incorporates industry-specific requirements

## The Prompt

```
**TASK:**
Create a detailed outline for a pipeline expansion proposal to increase crude oil takeaway capacity from the Permian Basin to Gulf Coast refineries. The outline should be suitable for board-level approval and include all sections needed for a $500M+ capital project decision.

**CONTEXT:**
I am a Business Development Director preparing a proposal for our company's largest capital investment in five years. The board meets quarterly and requires comprehensive analysis for projects over $100M. This expansion would add 200,000 barrels per day of capacity through a 36-inch pipeline spanning 450 miles. We need board approval at the Q3 meeting to begin construction in Q1 2026.

**REFERENCES:**
Structure the proposal outline with these major sections and subsections:

1. Executive Summary (1 page limit)
2. Market Opportunity
   - Permian production forecasts
   - Refinery demand analysis
   - Competitor pipeline capacity
   - Basis differential trends
3. Project Overview
   - Route selection and alternatives
   - Technical specifications
   - Environmental considerations
   - Right-of-way acquisition status
4. Financial Analysis
   - Capital cost breakdown
   - Operating expense projections
   - Revenue model (firm transportation agreements)
   - NPV, IRR, and payback period
   - Sensitivity analysis
5. Risk Assessment
   - Regulatory/permitting risks
   - Construction risks
   - Market risks
   - Mitigation strategies
6. Execution Plan
   - Project timeline with key milestones
   - Contractor selection process
   - Major equipment procurement
   - Commissioning plan
7. Strategic Alignment
   - Fit with company growth strategy
   - Synergies with existing assets
   - ESG considerations
8. Recommendation and Next Steps

Include estimated page counts and key graphics/tables needed for each section.
Add appendices list for supporting documents.
```

## Evaluation

To evaluate the outline quality:
- **Comprehensiveness** - Does it cover all board decision criteria?
- **Logic flow** - Does the structure build a compelling case?
- **Balance** - Appropriate detail level for $500M decision?
- **Practicality** - Can team realistically complete by Q3?
- **Clarity** - Would board members understand the proposal structure?

## Iteration

To enhance the outline:
- **Need more financial detail?** Add "Include subsection on financing options and impact on credit metrics"
- **Environmental focus?** Expand "Add dedicated section on carbon footprint and energy transition alignment"
- **Competition concerns?** Add "Include competitive intelligence on announced projects"
- **Partner involvement?** Include "Add section on potential JV partners and commercial structures"
- **Phasing options?** Request "Include alternative scenarios for phased construction"
