# Building Presentations Prompt Guide: Quarterly Operations Review

## Summary

**Building Presentations with M365 Copilot**  
Learn how to create professional, data-driven PowerPoint presentations for executive briefings in the midstream oil and gas industry.

## Explanation

This prompt demonstrates how to build a comprehensive quarterly operations review presentation using M365 Copilot's PowerPoint capabilities.

The prompt works because it:
- Specifies exact slide structure and content needs
- Leverages Copilot's ability to create visual slides
- Integrates data from multiple sources
- Requests appropriate formatting for executive audiences
- Includes speaker notes for presenters

## The Prompt

```
**TASK:**
Create a 12-slide PowerPoint presentation for our Q1 2025 Quarterly Operations Review with the Board of Directors. Include professional visuals, data charts, and comprehensive speaker notes for each slide.

**CONTEXT:**
I am the VP of Operations preparing to present our quarterly performance to the board next week. The presentation needs to cover operational highlights, safety metrics, financial performance, and strategic initiatives. Board members expect concise, visual slides with detailed backup information in speaker notes. Our operations include crude oil pipelines, NGL fractionation, and storage terminals across Texas and Louisiana.

**REFERENCES:**
Create the following slides:

1. Title Slide: "Q1 2025 Operations Review" with date and presenter info
2. Executive Summary: 4 key achievements and 2 challenges (bullet points with icons)
3. Safety Performance: 
   - TRIR trend chart (target: <0.5, actual: 0.42)
   - Days without incident counter
   - Safety initiatives progress
4. Pipeline Operations:
   - Volume chart by product (crude: 2.1M bpd, NGL: 850K bpd, refined: 1.3M bpd)
   - Utilization rates map
   - Uptime metrics (target: 98.5%, actual: 97.8%)
5. Fractionation Performance:
   - Throughput vs capacity chart
   - Product yield optimization results
   - Margin trends graph
6. Storage & Terminals:
   - Capacity utilization by location (Houston: 94%, Beaumont: 87%, Lake Charles: 91%)
   - Tank turns analysis
   - Customer satisfaction scores
7. Financial Highlights:
   - EBITDA waterfall chart
   - Opex per barrel trend
   - Capital efficiency metrics
8. Maintenance & Reliability:
   - Planned vs unplanned downtime
   - PM compliance percentage
   - Major turnaround schedule
9. Strategic Projects Update:
   - Permian expansion timeline
   - Digital transformation milestones
   - ESG initiatives progress
10. Market Conditions:
    - Crude differentials impact
    - Competitor activity summary
    - Demand outlook
11. Q2 2025 Priorities: 5 key focus areas with success metrics
12. Questions slide with contact information

Use our corporate template with blue/gray color scheme. Include data visualizations, not just text. Add speaker notes with talking points, key statistics, and transition phrases.
```

## Evaluation

To evaluate presentation quality:
- **Visual impact** - Are slides visually engaging with appropriate charts/graphics?
- **Data clarity** - Can board members quickly grasp key metrics?
- **Story flow** - Does the narrative build logically?
- **Speaker notes** - Sufficient detail for smooth delivery?
- **Professional polish** - Consistent formatting and corporate branding?

## Iteration

To improve the presentation:
- **Too text-heavy?** Add "Maximum 6 bullet points per slide, use more visuals"
- **Need more analysis?** Include "Add YoY comparison callouts on all metrics"
- **Board focus?** Specify "Emphasize ROI and shareholder value creation"
- **Missing context?** Add "Include industry benchmarks where available"
- **Time constraints?** Note "Design for 20-minute presentation with 10-minute Q&A"
