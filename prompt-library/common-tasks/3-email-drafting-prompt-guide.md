# Email Drafting Prompt Guide: Unplanned Outage Notification

## Summary

**Drafting Emails with M365 Copilot**  
Learn how to quickly draft professional, comprehensive emails for critical operational communications in the midstream oil and gas industry.

## Explanation

This prompt demonstrates how to draft a time-sensitive operational email regarding an unplanned outage using M365 Copilot.

The prompt works because it:
- Provides all critical information upfront
- Specifies the appropriate tone and urgency level
- Defines the audience and their concerns
- Leverages M365's integration with Outlook
- Includes compliance and legal considerations

## The Prompt

```
**TASK:**
Draft a professional email notifying customers about an unplanned outage at our Beaumont crude oil terminal. The email should be informative, apologetic where appropriate, and provide clear next steps. Include subject line and organize with clear sections.

**CONTEXT:**
I am the Terminal Operations Manager responding to a pump station failure that occurred at 14:30 today. We need to notify all affected customers within the next hour to maintain trust and comply with our service agreements. The terminal handles 350,000 barrels per day, and this outage affects 12 customers with active delivery schedules. Our engineering team estimates repairs will take 48-72 hours.

**REFERENCES:**
Include the following information:
- Incident time: Today at 14:30 CST
- Location: Beaumont Terminal, Pump Station 3
- Cause: Mechanical failure in primary pump motor (under investigation)
- Impact: All crude oil receipts suspended; withdrawals continue at 60% capacity
- Estimated restoration: 48-72 hours (by Friday 17:00 CST)
- Alternative arrangements: Nederland terminal can accept limited volumes
- Force majeure: May apply pending investigation

Email structure:
1. Subject line with "URGENT" flag
2. Brief opening with immediate impact statement
3. Detailed status section
4. Customer impact and alternatives
5. Timeline and updates schedule
6. Contact information for questions
7. Closing with commitment to safety and reliability

Tone: Professional, factual, and solution-focused. Express regret for inconvenience without admitting liability.
```

## Evaluation

To evaluate the email quality:
- **Clarity** - Is the operational impact immediately clear?
- **Completeness** - Are all customer concerns addressed?
- **Tone** - Professional yet empathetic without legal exposure?
- **Actionability** - Clear next steps for customers?
- **Compliance** - Includes required notifications and disclaimers?

## Iteration

To improve the email:
- **Too technical?** Add "Use terms familiar to crude oil shippers, not engineers"
- **Need urgency?** Specify "For customers with deliveries scheduled within 24 hours"
- **Legal concerns?** Add "Include standard force majeure language from our MSA"
- **Missing data?** Request "Add table showing each customer's allocated capacity at Nederland"
- **Follow-up needed?** Add "Include commitment to update every 12 hours"
