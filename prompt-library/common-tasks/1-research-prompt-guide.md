# Research Prompt Guide: Pipeline Integrity Management

## Summary

**Doing Research with M365 Copilot**  
Learn how to craft effective research prompts using Google's prompt framework to gather comprehensive information about complex operational topics in the midstream oil and gas industry.

## Explanation

This prompt demonstrates how to conduct thorough research on pipeline integrity management using M365 Copilot.

The prompt works because it:
- Gives Copilot explicit instructions with clear labels
- Provides rich context to generate relevant, industry-specific content
- Leverages M365's strength in creating formatted documents
- Includes specific examples and constraints to guide the output

## The Prompt

```
**TASK:**
Research current best practices for pipeline integrity management in midstream operations. Create a comprehensive Word document overview with executive summary, main sections with headers, bullet points for key findings, a comparison table, and recommendations.

**CONTEXT:**
I am a pipeline integrity engineer responsible for managing a network of crude oil and NGL pipelines in the Gulf Coast region. I need to present an updated integrity management strategy to senior management that balances safety, regulatory compliance, and cost-effectiveness. Our pipeline network includes both older steel pipelines (20-40 years) and newer installations.

**REFERENCES:**
Focus your research on:
- Inline inspection (ILI) technologies: MFL (Magnetic Flux Leakage), UT (Ultrasonic), EMAT (Electromagnetic Acoustic Transducer)
- Regulatory standards: PHMSA regulations, API Standard 1163, ASME B31.8S
- Risk factors: product type variations, pipeline age/material, operating pressures, soil conditions, population density
- Smart pigging advances and data analytics platforms
- Industry benchmarks for inspection intervals (typically 5-7 years for hazardous liquid pipelines)
- Budget constraints: typical ILI runs cost $1,000-$3,000 per mile

Structure the document with these sections:
1. Executive Summary (1 paragraph)
2. Current ILI Technologies Overview
3. Regulatory Requirements and Standards
4. Risk-Based Inspection Methodology
5. Cost-Benefit Analysis Framework
6. Emerging Technologies and Future Trends
7. Recommendations for Our Pipeline Network
```

## Evaluation

To evaluate the quality of Copilot's response, check for:
- **Completeness** - Does it address all requested sections?
- **Accuracy** - Are regulatory references and technical details correct?
- **Relevance** - Is the content specific to Gulf Coast crude/NGL operations?
- **Practicality** - Are recommendations actionable and cost-conscious?
- **Format** - Is the document well-structured and executive-ready?

## Iteration

If the initial response needs improvement, try these iterations:
- **Too generic?** Add more specific details about your pipeline characteristics (diameter, pressure ratings, specific products)
- **Missing recent developments?** Add "Include developments from 2023-2025" to the References section
- **Need more cost detail?** Specify budget ranges and ask for ROI calculations
- **Too technical?** Add "written for executive audience" to the Context
- **Need local focus?** Include specific Gulf Coast challenges (hurricanes, soil conditions, offshore connections)
